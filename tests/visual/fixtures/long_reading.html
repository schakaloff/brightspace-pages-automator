Cybersecurity Risk Management

Why risk management matters

Risk management helps organizations make informed decisions about systems, information, people, and business priorities. The goal is not to eliminate every risk, but to understand which risks require treatment.

A useful assessment identifies assets, threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, impact, existing controls, and responsible owners. Documenting these elements makes the final decision easier to explain and review.

A repeatable process

  1. Establish the context and scope.
  2. Identify assets and possible threats.
  3. Evaluate likelihood and impact.
  4. Select, document, and monitor controls.

Return to the assessment when technology, business processes, regulations, or threat conditions change.
